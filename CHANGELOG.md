# Revision history

Git records *what changed in a file*. This file records *why the history changed* —
which record arrived, which claim moved tier, which earlier statement turned out to
be wrong. Only the second is meaningful to a researcher.

The underlying evidence logs are held privately in the research project and are
deliberately not published. Claim IDs (`W-###`) and record IDs (`DRAFT-0003`,
`MISCW-0008`) refer to those logs.

---

## Revision 1 — 23 August 2026

First publication of this account. Eleven narrative pages plus a reference chart.

This is a second telling of evidence first published as *Three Sergeants*. It differs
in three deliberate ways:

- **It begins with the family, not the war.** The organising figure is Walter Newton
  Bryant, the eldest of three brothers and the one who stayed home — and therefore the
  only reason there is anyone here to ask the questions.
- **It withholds the events of 7 October 1918** until the October chapter, because
  that is the order in which the compiler learned them. The Sources page does not
  withhold, and says so at the top.
- **It opens and closes at a fireplace in Chase City in about 1970**, where two
  children met David Harrison Bryant and were told almost nothing.

**New evidence in this revision**

- **`DRAFT-0003`** — the draft registration card of **Walter Newton Bryant**, 5 June
  1917. Establishes a *third* brother, registering at the same precinct before the
  same registrar on the same day as David and Alexander; aged 28, married with two
  children, farming and saw-milling for W. J. Bryant & Sons, and himself a former
  Private of 2½ years in the Virginia State Guards. All three brothers claimed
  exemption. His was allowed. *(W-080, W-081, W-082, W-083)*
- **`MISCW-0008`** — **Jodie Sweat's written recollection of Uncle Dave**, 22 August
  2026, holograph with transcript. First-hand eyewitness testimony to the c.1970 visit
  from the second child in the room, recorded independently of the compiler's own
  memory. Together the two accounts establish a negative finding: David Bryant did not
  mention his brother on that occasion. *(W-085)*
- **`MAPW-0003`** — modern terrain and elevation map covering Châtel-Chéhéry,
  Apremont and Exermont. Shows why an advance across the Aire valley floor was
  overlooked from both flanks. Labelled as modern throughout.
- **`MISCW-0009`** — the 80th Division insignia scheme: shape by regiment, colour by
  battalion. Company D of the 317th was a red diamond. Provenance not yet confirmed
  and the page says so. *(W-084)*

**Carried forward from the companion account**

All corrections previously established remain in force and are listed on the Sources
page: the identity tags on the marker rather than with the body; three burials, not
four; serial 1817616; "gunshot wound in head"; the religious-belief question; Kokos's
death on 30 June 1958; the Company L commander who was never a casualty; and the 1st
Battalion on 4 October.

## How to read a revision entry

- **New evidence** — a record arrived and something can now be said that could not be
  said before.
- **Question closed** — an open question was resolved. The old state is described, not
  erased.
- **Corrections** — this account previously said something wrong. What it said and
  what it now says are both given. Corrections are never made silently.
- **Editorial**

- Authorship stated: **John Gladin**, with the written recollections of **Jodie Sweat**,
  who is credited by name on the start page and in the licence as the copyright holder
  of her own testimony.
- Family spelling **"Ma Mae"** confirmed for Virginia Mae Bryant. Jodie's verbatim
  quotation preserves her own spelling, "Ma mae", because it is a quotation. — presentation, licensing or structure, not bearing on the evidence.
